The Group

We buy what we understand, run what we own, and part with it only when someone else can take it further.

Founded in 2019 with a single freehold on the Sussex coast, Castle Horizon has grown into a family of operating companies spanning housing, care, development and technology. The group is owner-led and remotely run: the decisions that matter are taken by the people whose capital is at stake.

What we do

Four disciplines, one balance sheet.

AcquireHoldSell

Property investment

Freehold residential and mixed-use buildings on England's south coast, refurbished to an institutional standard and let on long, secure terms. We acquire directly, hold patiently, and sell whole portfolios when the capital is better deployed elsewhere.

Buy-sideSell-side

Business acquisitions

Established, owner-managed companies whose founders care about what happens next. We buy for the long term and structure around the founder's priorities — and, as owners who have sold, we understand what it takes to sell well.

ConversionAdapted homes

Development

Period buildings converted into accessible, adapted homes, and new units delivered on land we already own. The next chapter is a nationwide programme of adapted housing across the United Kingdom.

CareTechnology

Operating platforms

The companies that make the assets work: a supported-accommodation operator working alongside local authorities, a care platform in formation, and an in-house technology company that builds the group's own systems and offers them to others.

How we work

Quiet, aligned, on the ground.

Discretion

Nothing leaves the room.

Names, numbers and intentions travel under NDA or not at all. We have been on the receiving end of loose talk in a small market and we do not pass it on.

Alignment

You deal with the owners.

We do not manage other people's money. There is no mandate to serve, no fee to earn on the way through — only the deal itself, and whether it is right for both sides.

Stewardship

Leave it better.

We refurbish to the standard we would live in, house people others find hard to house, and hand every building and business on in better shape than we found it.
