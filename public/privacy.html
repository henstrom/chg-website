Privacy

How we handle your information.

Castle Horizon Ltd ("we") is the controller of personal information collected through this website and through enquiries made to us.

What we collect

When you contact us by email, telephone or the enquiry form we receive the details you choose to give us — typically your name, organisation, contact details and the substance of your enquiry. If you sign our non-disclosure agreement online we also record your typed signature, job title, the time of signing and the IP address it was signed from, as evidence of the agreement, and we keep that record for the life of the agreement. This website does not use analytics cookies or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and reliability.

Why we use it

To respond to your enquiry, to conduct any resulting discussions or transactions, and to meet our legal obligations, including anti-money-laundering checks where a transaction proceeds. Our lawful bases are our legitimate interest in running the business and, where relevant, the performance of a contract or compliance with law.

Confidential enquiries

Information you share with us about a business or property you may wish to sell is treated as confidential from the first contact, and is shared within the group and with our professional advisers only as far as is necessary to evaluate it. Where a non-disclosure agreement is signed, its terms take precedence.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We share it with our professional advisers (legal, tax, accounting) and service providers (email and hosting) under confidentiality, and with authorities where required by law. We keep enquiry records for as long as a relationship or legal obligation reasonably requires, and delete them thereafter.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our use of it. Write to hello@chg.global. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
