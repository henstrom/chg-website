The offering

What a buyer acquires.

A single, consolidated ownership of period residential buildings within one connected sub-region of England's south coast. Every building has been refurbished by the group to the standard it operates itself, and the whole is let to one established operating tenant — the group's own housing business — on a long, full repairing and insuring lease with upward-only reviews.

The result is institutional-grade income from an asset class with structural demand: well-managed supported accommodation, placed and funded by local authorities, in a part of the country where such homes are scarce. The buyer acquires the freeholds and the lease; the operator, its staff and its council relationships stay exactly where they are.

The portfolio is available as a whole to a single buyer, or to a joint-venture partner alongside the founders, who would retain a minority interest. Share sale and asset sale structures are both open.

Why it is for sale

Capital for the next chapter.

The group is selling to free capital for the nationwide expansion of its adapted-housing programme across the United Kingdom.

This is not an exit from the sector. The founders remain shareholders in the operating business, remain personally involved in its day-to-day running, and that business remains the tenant. A buyer therefore acquires an income stream from a counterparty with every reason to see the buildings succeed — and, should they wish, a partner who knows each building down to the last fitting.

Because the tenant is ours, we can be unusually clear about the operating reality behind the rent: occupancy, council relationships, maintenance history and the pipeline of further units already consented within the portfolio.

Process

Four steps, no auction.

We are speaking to a small number of qualified buyers directly. There is no broker-led process and no public marketing of the buildings themselves.

I

Introduction

A conversation with the owner. If there is a fit, a short mutual NDA follows within days.

II

Information memorandum

Full particulars under NDA: the buildings, the lease, the tenant's operating record and the financials — with the transaction adviser available for questions.

III

Visits and management

Site inspections and meetings with the operating management team, arranged discreetly around the residents.

IV

Offer and exclusivity

Indicative offer, agreed heads of terms and a period of exclusivity for legal and technical due diligence.
