About the group

Built on the ground, not the spreadsheet.

Castle Horizon began with one freehold on the Sussex coast and a simple idea: own the asset, run the operation, and answer for both.

The story

From one building to a group.

The first acquisition, in late 2019, was a period building that most investors would have walked past. Refurbished properly and let on secure terms, it became the model: buy well, build to a standard, operate ourselves, and hold.

That model repeated along the south coast until the property and the operating business had each become companies in their own right. Around them grew a development arm converting period buildings into adapted, accessible homes; a care platform now in formation; and a technology company that started by building the group's own systems and now offers them beyond it.

Today the group is led from Brighton, Dubai and Oslo by its founders, who remain personally involved in the operating businesses. The next chapter is a nationwide programme of adapted housing across the United Kingdom — which is why, for the first time, we are also sellers.
